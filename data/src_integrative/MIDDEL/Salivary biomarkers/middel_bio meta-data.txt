Variables:

pid - participant ID
group - allocated group (GMT, RCS, CONTROL)
month - month after randomisation (1, 3, 6)
time - time point in relation to session (a - before; b - after; c - 60min after session)
sAA - salivary amylase (note: correction factor for salivettes has already been applied)
sCort - salivary cortisol
vas_s - visual analog scale for stress
present_sess - indicating whether participant attended the session (1 - yes; 2 - no; 3 or NA - unknown)
